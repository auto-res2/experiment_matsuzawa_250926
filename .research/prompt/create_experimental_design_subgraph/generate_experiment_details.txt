# Experiment Strategy
────────────────────────────────────────
EXPERIMENT 1 – Large-scale Benchmarks

Goal: Show that VNP yields consistent accuracy gains over strong baselines and recent SOTA on a variety of few-shot tasks.

Protocol
1. Datasets
   • In-domain: miniImageNet, tieredImageNet, CIFAR-FS (standard 5-way 1/5-shot).
   • Cross-domain: Meta-Dataset split A→B (train on ImageNet + OmniGlot, test on CUB, Fungi, Aircraft, QuickDraw). These test sets differ strongly in intra-class variance.
2. Models
   • Backbone: ResNet-12 frozen after supervised pre-training on each dataset’s base classes.
   • Classifiers: (i) Plain ProtoNet; (ii) VNP (β=1); (iii) state-of-the-art metric learners that incur minimal extra training cost (e.g. Cosine ProtoNet, Covariance ProtoNet).
3. Evaluation
   • 10 000 episodes per dataset; 95 % CI.
   • GPU: single A100 (
80 GB); all runs <2 h per dataset.

Expected outcome
   • In-domain: +1–2 pp accuracy over plain ProtoNet on all datasets, matching or slightly exceeding SOTA without extra parameters.
   • Cross-domain: bigger gains (+2–4 pp) because support scatter varies widely between natural and sketch images.
Demonstrates that a variance-aware normalisation improves generalisation broadly and is especially valuable when class reliability differs across domains.

────────────────────────────────────────
EXPERIMENT 2 – Scatter-Controlled Robustness Test

Goal: Provide causal evidence that VNP specifically counteracts prototype unreliability.

Protocol
1. Take a held-out validation set (e.g. miniImageNet val).
2. For every episode, synthetically add zero-mean Gaussian noise η~𝒩(0,σ²I) to the support embeddings only; vary σ so that class scatter inflation factors are {1×,2×,3×,5×}.
3. Keep the query features clean; classify with ProtoNet and VNP.
4. Repeat for 5 000 episodes per noise level.

Expected outcome
   • Baseline accuracy degrades roughly linearly with σ.
   • VNP curve is noticeably flatter; relative gain rises from ≈+1 pp (no noise) to >+6 pp (5× noise).
Demonstrates that VNP is robust to unreliable prototypes and that the benefit grows with scatter, isolating the very mechanism the method addresses.

────────────────────────────────────────
EXPERIMENT 3 – Ablation & Calibration Study

Goal: Verify that the β exponent controls the trade-off between bias and variance, and show that VNP improves probabilistic calibration without computational penalty.

Protocol
1. Keep miniImageNet test episodes fixed.
2. Sweep β∈{0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1.0}.
3. Metrics
   • Accuracy.
   • Expected Calibration Error (ECE) and negative log-likelihood (NLL) on query predictions.
   • Throughput (episodes/s) and peak GPU memory.

Expected outcome
   • Accuracy follows a shallow concave curve, peaking near β≈0.75–1.0.
   • ECE and NLL monotonically improve with larger β, indicating better confidence estimates.
   • Throughput and memory identical (±1 %) across β values, proving negligible overhead.
Shows (a) why β≈1 is sensible, (b) that VNP yields better-calibrated probabilities—not just higher accuracy—and (c) that the method is essentially cost-free.
────────────────────────────────────────
